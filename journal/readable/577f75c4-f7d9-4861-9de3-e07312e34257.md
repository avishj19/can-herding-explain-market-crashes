## A science caveat to know before demo day

The model was fitted to the S&P 500. The S&P 500 is an **index**: an average of 500 companies, so one company's news mostly cancels out. A single stock also has its own news, such as earnings reports, lawsuits or a CEO quitting. Those news jumps aren't the bell-curve news your model assumes.

So when a stock doesn't match the model, that's often a real finding, not a bug. It's also why your 20 saved stocks were chosen with **market beta** between 0.9 and 1.1. Beta measures how much a stock moves with the whole market; between 0.9 and 1.1 means they move about like the market itself. The page would say this next to the comparison.
